BIRC Conference 2026

Set to take place at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi

Bharat International Rice Conference (BIRC) 2026 is a landmark global platform dedicated to India's most vital agri-export, rice, jointly organised by APEDA and the Indian Rice Exporters Federation (IREF).

India, the world's largest exporter of rice, exported 20.19 million metric tonnes in 2024–25. BIRC 2026 is the platform to explore this momentum and unlock new market potential.
