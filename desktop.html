Built differently

What Makes the BIRC 2026 Knowledge Sessions Different

EY Post-Conference Report

The Ernst & Young Post-Conference Report on BIRC 2025 established the 2025 edition as the world's largest-ever dedicated rice conference in terms of scale and participation.

EY BIRC 2025 Report

Not long speeches. Not boring panels. Real working sessions. Every BIRC 2026 session is built like a business training class, with months of planning behind each one, focused on the real decisions rice businesses make every day. And every speaker is chosen for one reason: they have actually done the business, not just studied it.

Every session answers one simple question: how will this grow your rice business? No heavy theory, no general talk, only the real decisions you face. Where prices will go. Which markets to target. How to lower risk, protect margins, use new ideas and find bigger partners. You go home with clear decisions, not just notes.

Price affects every decision: buying, stocking, contracts and exports. So BIRC 2026 makes price forecasting the main highlight: crop surveys, production estimates, demand-and-supply data and expert views, all in one place. Understand the market before it moves.

Knowledge is useful only when it makes money. BIRC 2026 turns learning into results: real case studies, live market analysis and simple business examples that improve your planning, strengthen your position and protect your profit across the value chain.

Big sales do not always mean big profit, margins do. BIRC 2026 focuses on what really affects your earnings: price ups and downs, payment and transaction risk, extra costs and trade planning. Protect your money, keep your margins and grow stronger.

The next big market is not always where everyone is looking. BIRC 2026 looks ahead: new countries, changing customer tastes, new trade rules and government schemes, so you can enter early, move first and grow where the demand is going.

New technology is useful only if you can actually use it. We checked hundreds of new products, technologies and solutions, and picked only the ones with real business value, shown live at the Top Innovations Reveal and Innovation Showcase. Practical, profitable and ready to use.

Rice is not just a commodity, it is loved on plates all over the world. Nine special Experience Zones and a Global Rice Cuisine Showcase bring rice dishes from more than 50 countries and over 25 types of biryani to life. See the demand for yourself, and find your next product, market or value-added idea.

Good deals should not depend on luck or a chance meeting. BIRC 2026 makes networking planned and simple: app-based scheduling and organised B2B meetings connect buyers, exporters, millers and importers with the right partners. Less waiting, more real deals.
