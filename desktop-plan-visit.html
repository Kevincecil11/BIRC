Travel

How to Reach Bharat Mandapam.

Pragati Maidan, Mathura Road, New Delhi 110001. Delegate entry is Gate 10.

Fastest and most convenient

The Blue Line drops you within a 5 to 8 minute walk of the delegate gate.

Key stations

Supreme Court Station

Blue Line · 1.2 km · 5-7 min walk

Exit Gate 1, walk via Mathura Road, enter Gate 10.

Before You Travel.

International delegates need a valid Indian business visa. BIRC issues invitation letters on request once your registration is confirmed.

Accommodation is not included with standard passes. Partner hotels offer discounted delegate rates, and select sponsorship tiers include hotel stays.

Business formal for conference sessions and ceremonies. Business casual is acceptable on the exhibition floor.
