One Grain Can Carry a Civilisation.

Rice is nourishment, ritual, labour, landscape and identity. The BIRC Rice Art Showcase invites artists, makers, studios, designers and creative businesses to reveal what the world's most shared grain can become in the hands of imagination.

How to take advantage

Arrive With Art. Leave With Momentum.

The strongest showcase is not only a display. It is a story, a conversation starter and a bridge to whatever you want to create next.

01 · Before BIRC

Build the Story

Define the rice connection, show the transformation through process material, and prepare the next step you want from the showcase.

02 · At BIRC

Make the Work Easy to Understand

Bring process photos, samples or a short making film so audiences can see how rice becomes the final piece.

03 · After BIRC

Carry the Momentum Forward

Use the international showcase moment for commissions, orders, collaborations, media coverage and future exhibitions.

One simple test

Does Rice Make It Possible?

Does rice — or any part, by-product, story or material connected to rice — make your work possible? Then this opportunity is for you.

Free to apply. Shortlisted artists are contacted with display and participation details.
