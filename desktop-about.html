Who we are

Our Story

From Vision to Reality

BIRC's journey began with a simple goal: to give India's rice industry a platform worthy of its global scale. Founded through a partnership between APEDA and the Indian Rice Exporters Federation (IREF).

Today, BIRC has grown into the definitive gathering for the global rice trade, connecting thousands of delegates and buyers from over 120 countries.

The ecosystem

Industry Overview

India is the world's largest exporter of rice, shipping over 20 million metric tonnes annually across Basmati and Non-Basmati varieties to more than 150 countries.

The industry sits at the intersection of agriculture, trade policy, and global commerce, shaped by APEDA, the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, and State Governments.

BIRC 2026 brings this entire ecosystem together, connecting producers, processors, exporters, and policymakers.
